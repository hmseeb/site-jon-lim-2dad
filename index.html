Skilled nursing at home

Stay out of the hospital and keep a clinician watching the details that matter.

A nurse comes to the home to assess the patient, care for wounds, manage medications and injections, check vitals, and teach the family what to watch for between visits. Findings go back to the patient’s physician so the plan of care keeps up with how they are actually doing.

  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Medication setup, teaching and injections
  • Vital signs, disease monitoring and physician reporting
  • Catheter, ostomy and diabetic care

Good to know

Visits are scheduled with the household, not around it. If a day needs to move, we re-confirm with you directly rather than leaving the appointment in question.

Physical therapy

Walk, stand and climb your own stairs again without fear of falling.

Strength, balance and mobility work done in the rooms and doorways the patient actually uses, with a home exercise program the family can carry on between visits.

02

Occupational therapy

Get back to dressing, bathing and cooking without waiting for help.

Training in daily activities, safe transfers, energy conservation and adaptive equipment, plus practical changes to the home that remove the obstacles causing the most trouble.

03

Speech therapy

Be understood again — and eat and drink safely.

Evaluation and treatment for speech, language, cognition and swallowing, including diet texture guidance and strategies the family can use at every meal.

04

Medical social services

Stop guessing which agency, form or benefit comes next.

A medical social worker helps the household sort out community resources, equipment, long-term planning and the paperwork that tends to stall care at home.

05

Home health aide support

Keep personal care dignified when family can’t be there for every hour.

Hands-on help with bathing, grooming, dressing and mobility under the supervision of the nurse, coordinated with the rest of the plan of care.

Pediatric home health

Care for children, delivered at home and around the school day.

Visita Home Health works with infants, children and teens who need clinical care outside a hospital. Visits are scheduled around school, therapy and the rest of family life, and parents are taught every step so they are never left doing something unfamiliar alone.

Request a home visit

Pediatric skilled nursing

In-home nursing visits for children with medical needs, including assessments, medication administration, wound care and teaching for parents and caregivers.

Child-focused therapy

Physical, occupational and speech therapy for children, carried out in the home using the toys, routines and spaces the child already knows.

Family training

Step-by-step coaching for parents and guardians on equipment, feeding, positioning and what to watch for between visits.

Coordination with the child’s doctor

Visit findings and progress reported back to the pediatrician or specialist so the plan of care reflects how the child is really doing.
